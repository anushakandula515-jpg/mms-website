Account deletion

Delete your My Montessori Shelf account

App: My Montessori Shelf · Contact: support@mymontessorishelf.app

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open My Montessori Shelf.
  2. Press and hold the 👪 button to open the parents' area.
  3. Scroll to your account and tap Delete my account and data.
  4. Confirm twice. If Google asks you to sign in again, do so; it is a safety check.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email support@mymontessorishelf.app with the subject "Delete my account", from the Google email address you signed in with. We will delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account: name, email address, profile photo and sign-in user ID (from Google Firebase Authentication).
  • When you delete in the app: your child's progress, settings and saved voice clips on that phone.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. Voice clips on our server are stored by sentence (for example "Take a mat from the basket"), not by person, so they are not linked to you and are kept to serve all families. Our providers' short-lived security logs expire on their own schedules.
