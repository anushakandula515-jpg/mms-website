Privacy policy

Your child's privacy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short: A parent signs in once with Google, so we receive the parent's name, email address and profile photo. We use them only to keep your classroom yours. We collect nothing about your child: their progress stays on your phone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share your information. You can delete your account and data at any time, in the app or by email.

Who we are

My Montessori Shelf ("the app", "we", "us") is a Montessori learning app for children aged 1½–6. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. It applies to the Android app with the package name com.mymontessorishelf.app and to this website. Contact: support@mymontessorishelf.app.

Information we collect

From the parent, when signing in with Google:

  • name, email address and profile photo (as shown on the Google account),
  • a sign-in user ID created by Firebase Authentication.

We use these only to set up and recognise your account, to show you who is signed in, and to let only your app use the guide's voice. We do not use them for advertising or marketing, and we do not send newsletters unless you ask for them.

From your child: nothing. The app does not ask for or collect a child's name, age, photo, voice, location, contacts or any other personal information. Sign-in is done by the parent, once, on a screen meant for grown-ups.

What stays on your device

To work like a real classroom, the app remembers a few things on your device only: the age group you chose, which works have been done and how they went (presented, practising, mastered), simple settings such as session length, the classroom plant, and voice clips it has already played. This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage, or deleting your account in the app removes it.

If Android's own backup to your Google account is turned on, Android may include the app's settings in that backup. That backup is made and controlled by Android and Google under your Google account, not by us, and we cannot see it.

The guide's voice

Instructions are read aloud in a human-sounding voice. To make a clip, the app sends the sentence to be spoken (fixed app text such as "Take a mat from the basket") together with the parent's sign-in token to our voice server. The server checks the token, then gets the audio from our text-to-speech provider. The sentence never contains personal information. The token is checked and not stored. Clips are saved by sentence, not by person, so they cannot be linked back to you. If a clip can't be fetched, the phone's own text-to-speech voice is used instead. The app never records or listens to your child.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the app. They process information only on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase Authentication (Google LLC): parent sign-in. Stores the sign-in details listed above.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.): runs our voice server. Like any web service, it may keep short-lived technical logs such as IP addresses for security.
  • Sarvam AI (Sarvam AI Pvt. Ltd., India): turns app sentences into speech. It receives only the sentence text, never your name, email or any information about your child.

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone else.

Security

All information sent by the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The voice server accepts requests only from signed-in parents of this app.

How long we keep information

We keep the parent's sign-in details while the account exists. When you delete your account in the app, they are deleted from Firebase straight away. If you ask us by email, we delete them within 30 days.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open the parents' area (press and hold the 👪 button) and choose Delete my account and data. This deletes your sign-in account and everything the app keeps on the phone. You can also ask us to do it: see how to delete your account, or email support@mymontessorishelf.app from the email address you signed in with.

Permissions

The app uses the internet for sign-in and to fetch voice clips, and vibration (haptics) for gentle feedback when pieces are moved. It does not ask for the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

Advertising, analytics and links

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no social-media buttons. Links that leave the app (such as this policy) are only in the parents' area, which opens only with a press and hold.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for young children and follows Google Play's Families policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We believe our approach is consistent with children's privacy laws such as COPPA (United States), GDPR and UK GDPR, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Parents may contact us at any time to review or delete their information.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and it loads its fonts from this site itself. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. If you email us, for example to become an early tester, we use your email address only to reply to you, and we delete it when you ask.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at support@mymontessorishelf.app.
